Here is the list of climate action projects in South Africa that can be mapped to SDG target 13.3:

1. Project name: Active Climate Change Citizenship for a Just Transition in South Africa
Location: South Africa
Summary: This project aims to empower youth, women, and communities to harness opportunities within the renewable energy sector and provide tools to address climate change mitigation and adaptation.
Impact: The project will create awareness about the economic, environmental, and social benefits of renewable energy and career opportunities available to them in these fields.
Source: https://saclimatechamps.co.za/

2. Project name: Green Opportunities Challenge
Location: South Africa
Summary: This project aims to create awareness about the economic, environmental, and social benefits of renewable energy and career opportunities available to them in these fields.
Impact: The project will create a community of young people who have a voice to influence national and local climate and energy policy.
Source: https://saclimatechamps.co.za/

3. Project name: Renewable Energy Master Plan
Location: South Africa
Summary: This project aims to develop industrial value chains for renewable energy technologies to drive development and create inclusive jobs.
Impact: The project will accelerate the rollout of renewables and create a low-cost renewables-based electricity system.
Source: https://www.greenclimate.fund/countries/south-africa

4. Project name: Climate Change Bill
Location: South Africa
Summary: This project aims to make South Africa's NDC legally binding, requiring the government to set sectoral emission targets and allocate carbon budgets to significant GHG emitting companies.
Impact: The project will be a significant step forward for South Africa, making its NDC legally binding and requiring the government to set sectoral emission targets.
Source: https://www.greenclimate.fund/countries/south-africa

5. Project name: Just Energy Transition Investment Plan (JET IP)
Location: South Africa
Summary: This project aims to catalyze a sustained just energy transition in South Africa, with a focus on the power sector.
Impact: The project will support a transition away from coal and include support for impacted coal workers, with investments also in new energy vehicles and green hydrogen.
Source: https://www.greenclimate.fund/countries/south-africa

6. Project name: Climate Ambition to Accountability Project
Location: South Africa
Summary: This project aims to build capacity in a core group of civil society organizations and community-based organizations to train high school teachers and community organizations about renewable energy and climate change adaptations.
Impact: The project will create awareness about the economic, environmental, and social benefits of renewable energy and career opportunities available to them in these fields.
Source: https://www.wwf.org.za/our_work/initiatives/climate_ambition_to_accountability_project

7. Project name: Be Resilient South Africa Project
Location: South Africa
Summary: This project aims to introduce a novel, bottom-up participatory approach to climate change adaptation, providing an adequate planning pathway for communities.
Impact: The project will create a community of young people who have a voice to influence national and local climate and energy policy.
Source: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/be-resilient-south-africa-project

Location: South Africa
