Here is the list of engagements initiatives mapped to SDG target 13.3:

1. Project Name: Keep It Cool
Location: Eastern Cape, KwaZulu-Natal, Limpopo
Summary: The project integrated climate change education into the curriculum of one hundred secondary schools across five districts in Kwazulu-Natal, Limpopo, and Eastern Cape.
Impact: The project achieved its intended impact at all levels - school, community, district, and national. It influenced learners and communities, and even contributed to national meetings on integrating climate change in the curriculum.
SDG Map: SDG 13.3 - Improve education, awareness-raising and human and institutional capacity on climate change mitigation, adaptation, impact reduction and early warning
URL: https://www.vvob.org/en/programmes/south-africa-keep-it-cool-climate-change-education

2. Project Name: Climate Change Curriculum
Location: Western Cape
Summary: The Climate Change Curriculum will be launched at a workshop with educators in the Western Cape on Saturday, 25 February 2023. Training is accredited by the South African Council for Educators (SACE), and each educator will receive 10 Continued Professional Training and Development Points (CPTDP) after the workshop.
Impact: The Climate Change Curriculum will provide youth with the knowledge and skills to make informed decisions about how to adapt to climate change, and lead future discussions calling for climate justice.
SDG Map: SDG 13.3 - Improve education, awareness-raising and human and institutional capacity on climate change mitigation, adaptation, impact reduction and early warning
URL: https://trees.org.za/climate-change-curriculum-south-african-schools/

Location: Kwazulu-Natal, Limpopo, and Eastern Cape
Summary: This project integrated climate change education into the curriculum of one hundred secondary schools across five districts in Kwazulu-Natal, Limpopo, and Eastern Cape.
